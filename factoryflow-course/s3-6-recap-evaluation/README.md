## What you can now say you can do

You can take an undocumented script from a colleague who has left, make it reproducible, make it readable, prove it is correct, and hand it to someone else.

That is five things, and most people with a computer science degree cannot do all five. Write it down somewhere you will find it when you next update your CV.

## The one habit, if you remember nothing else

> **Commit small, run often, write down why.**

## Before you leave

- **Your repository is yours.** It is on your account. Nobody takes it away on Friday evening.
- Check it still clones and runs somewhere that is not your laptop.
- Take the URL of the course folder if you want the solutions.

## The evaluation

Fifteen minutes, written, anonymous. The parts that get read hardest are the ones about what should change. Be specific — "S2.3 moved too fast between the live build and the pairs block" is usable; "good workshop" is not.

## One next step

Pick one thing from the "what we did not cover" list and name the smallest version of it you could do in a weekend. Databases and SQL is the highest-value one for this cohort.

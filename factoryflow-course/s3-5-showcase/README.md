# S3.5 — Showcase

**Day 3 · 16:00–17:00 · 5 minutes demo, 2 minutes questions, per pair.**

## The brief

> Five minutes. You are showing this to the person who will have to use it on
> Monday.
>
> Start with the screen they will look at. Show it running. Tell us one thing
> you found in the data. Then tell us **one decision you made that you are not
> sure about.**

## A structure that works

| Time | What |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:30 | *"This is FactoryFlow. Here is what a supervisor sees."* Dashboard on screen. |
| 0:30–2:00 | Walk through the numbers. Not the code. |
| 2:00–3:00 | **One thing you found in the data.** |
| 3:00–4:00 | How you know it is right — run your tests live, run your container live. |
| 4:00–5:00 | **One decision you are not sure about.** |

Running your tests live in front of the room takes ten seconds and is the most
persuasive thing you can do. Do it.

## Before you present

- Have the dashboard already open and the API already running. Do not spend
  your first minute starting things.
- Decide who talks when.
- Know your answer to the question every pair gets asked:

> **"How do you know this number is right?"**

## When you are in the audience

You are a sceptical shift supervisor. You have question cards on your table —
ask at least one of every pair. Sceptical and fair.

The timer is hard. At 5:00 you stop, whatever you were saying.
